# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Purpose
Use this guide as the starting point for OctoAcme project management practices. It summarizes the core delivery lifecycle and links to the detailed process documents used by project, product, and delivery teams.

## Process summary
OctoAcme runs projects through a lightweight lifecycle: initiate the work, plan the delivery approach, execute in small increments, manage risks and communication throughout, release safely, and capture learnings for continuous improvement. Teams use shared roles, clear artifacts, and regular communication cadences to keep work aligned from idea to outcome.

## Process documents
- [Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md) — high-level principles, roles, lifecycle stages, and key artifacts.
- [Project Initiation Guide](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) — define business need, stakeholders, success metrics, and go/no-go decisions.
- [Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md) — build the backlog, align milestones, and document dependencies.
- [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) — manage delivery rhythm, project boards, pull requests, and quality checks.
- [Risk Management & Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) — maintain the risk register, status updates, and escalation paths.
- [Release & Deployment Guide](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) — prepare releases, deploy safely, and handle rollback scenarios.
- [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) — run retrospectives and track improvement actions.
- [Roles and Personas](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) — reference responsibilities and communication patterns for key contributors.

## Recommended reading order
1. Start with the [Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md).
2. Use [Project Initiation Guide](./octoacme-project-initiation.md) and [Project Planning](./octoacme-project-planning.md) when shaping new work.
3. Keep [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md) and [Risk Management & Communication](./octoacme-risks-and-communication.md) close during delivery.
4. Refer to [Release & Deployment Guide](./octoacme-release-and-deployment.md) before launch and [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md) after milestones.
